The Bias Engine

Every article is fetched in full, run through a transformer model, and scored across four lenses. Ownership carries the dominant weight (50%) because who owns the press is the most stable signal in this corpus.

WBI = 0.15·S  +  0.20·L  +  0.50·O  +  0.15·P

Full-body analysis. Each URL is dereferenced with trafilatura, so scoring runs on the complete article — not just the headline. Cached for an hour.

ML lexical & stance. Sentiment is scored by DistilBERT-SST-2; political stance by zero-shot DeBERTa-v3-MNLI against labels like "praises the ruling party", "criticises the opposition", "highlights wrongdoing". ML scores override the lexicon when present; the lexicon stays as a fallback.

S — Source weighting. Quoted sources tagged as Official, Independent, Affected. Ratio ≥ 2:1 triggers Access Journalism.

L — Lexical framing. Either the ML sentiment (if available) or the lexicon score, sharpened for volatility.

O — Ownership influence (dominant, always present). Each outlet maps to its parent group's sector portfolio. Even on stories that don't touch those sectors, the publisher's historical prior contributes a baseline — so every article carries an ownership weighting.

P — Partisan polarity. Ruling vs opposition mention × sentiment polarity. Subject-modulates the lexical score: criticism of the opposition reads as pro-establishment.

Volatility

Scores are sharpened with sign(x)·|x|^0.55 so mild signals become decisive. The publisher prior contributes only 15% — the engine's own signal dominates.

Bias Patterns

  • Communal Bias Nationalist labels (Urban Naxal, Anti-National) or caste signals fire.
  • Developmental Bias Ownership / sector match in infra-mining-energy, or critical keywords missing.
  • Access Journalism Official-to-Independent source ratio ≥ 2:1.
  • Linguistic Elitism Loaded vocabulary or a flagged headline-body gap.

Every lexicon, lookup table, and weight in this engine is editable. The output is a profile to argue with, not a verdict to defer to.
